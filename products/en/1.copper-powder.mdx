## Description

Ultrafine copper powder with chemical purity of at least **99.9999%**. Manufactured according to TU 24.44.21-002_75815004-2022.

Copper powder is used in the production of high-precision alloys, electronics, catalysts, and in research.

## Specifications

- **Purity:** 99.9999%
- **Packaging:** PET containers 250 g – 2000 g, glass ampoules 200 g
- **Standard:** TU 24.44.21-002_75815004-2022
- **Appearance:** fine reddish-pink powder

## Certification

All products are certified at the Laboratory of Mass Spectrometric and Ecoanalytical Methods, IASH GIREDMET (Moscow).

At the buyer's request, we certify products at the independent laboratory of the Institute of Rare Earth and Strategic Metals ISE AG (Switzerland). Giredmet certificate verification is available without re-analysis.
